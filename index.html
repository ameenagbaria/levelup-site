Meet your teacher · تعرّفوا على المعلّم

Ameen Agbaria

English has been part of my life since I was 5, when I started Helen Doron classes in my village. At 11 I took my first trip to the UK, and from then on I spent my summers in English-speaking countries. At 14 I spent a month in New York on my own, taking language courses. Outside of class, I read countless books and grew up on English shows, movies and music, so English became part of my everyday life.

In high school I founded a Model UN and Speech & Debate club, run entirely in English, and taught English to more than 50 students while I was still a student myself. That work earned me the Elad Riban Award for Outstanding Youth Volunteer. I competed at conferences in Israel and abroad, won an award at Yale Model United Nations in the US, joined Debate for Peace, and visited the United Nations on a study program, where I met with ambassadors.

I then spent four and a half years in the US, plus a semester in France, and graduated in Computer Science from Georgia Tech with highest honors. As Head Teaching Assistant, I taught and supported more than 3,000 students. Now I'm back home, helping students in our community build their English and reach their goals, while also working on my own projects in tech and business.

Parents ask us

أسئلة الأهل

Is my child too young to start?

Kids Club starts at 6. Younger kids learn through stories, songs and games, not grammar drills.

My child is behind at school. Can you help?

Yes. The free level test shows where the gaps are, and School Support works on the actual school material.

Will I know how my child is doing?

Every month you get a short WhatsApp report, and you can message the teacher any time.

Can we switch between cities?

Yes. Members can make up a missed class in any of our cities or online.
